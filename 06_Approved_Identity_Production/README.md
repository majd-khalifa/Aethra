# AETHRA — Approved Brand Identity Production Package

**Central Brand Idea:** *"From the unseen to the extraordinary."*  
**Production Directory:** `06_Approved_Identity_Production`

This directory contains the complete, production-ready brand identity system for **AETHRA**, reconstructed faithfully from `00_Reference/APPROVED_LOGO_REFERENCE.png` and executed across every required digital and physical touchpoint.

## Directory Structure

- **`00_Reference/`** — Original approved reference image (`APPROVED_LOGO_REFERENCE.png`), handoff instructions, and master specification.
- **`01_Logo/`**
  - `SVG/` — Canonical editable vector masters (stacked, horizontal, standalone symbol, wordmark, light/dark backgrounds, monochrome black/white, and micro-size optimized).
  - `PNG/` — Transparent and background-paired PNG exports at `512 px`, `1024 px`, `2048 px`, and `4096 px`.
  - `Monochrome/` — Dedicated 1-color ink (`#171327`) and reverse-white (`#FFFFFF`) vector & raster exports.
  - `App_Icons/` — Multi-resolution favicon and launcher icon suite (`16 px` to `1024 px`, light, dark, and circular social avatars).
  - `Fidelity_Proof/` — Pixel-grid A/B reconstruction proof (`ab_reference_vs_vector_proof.png`) and construction/clear-space/misuse specification sheet.
- **`02_Design_System/`** — Machine-readable tokens (`design_tokens.json`, `design_tokens.css`) and visual specification boards for Color, Typography, and Graphic Language & Layout Grids.
- **`03_Instagram/`** — Complete Instagram launch system: 6 coordinated `1080 × 1350` portrait posts, 4 `1080 × 1920` stories, 5 `1080 × 1080` highlight covers, profile avatars, bio copy guide, 3-post & 6-post grid previews, and editable SVG/HTML templates.
- **`04_Brand_Applications/`** — Website desktop (`1440 px`) & mobile (`390 px`) designs, Light & Dark software architecture dashboards (`1440 × 960`), mobile splash screens, front/back business cards, 16:9 presentation slide deck (`1920 × 1080`), A4 proposal cover & letterhead, and HTML email signature.
- **`05_Brand_Guidelines/`** — Complete brand guidelines in Markdown (`AETHRA_Brand_Guidelines.md`), interactive HTML (`AETHRA_Brand_Book.html`), multi-page PDF (`AETHRA_Brand_Book.pdf`), and bundled open-source `.ttf` font families (`Space Grotesk`, `Inter`, `IBM Plex Mono`).
- **`06_QA_and_Delivery/`** — Master overview contact sheet (`overview_contact_sheet.png`), light/dark consistency showcase (`light_and_dark_showcase.png`), A/B fidelity proof, `LOGO_FIDELITY_REVIEW.md`, `ASSET_MANIFEST.md`, and `QA_VALIDATION_REPORT.md`.
