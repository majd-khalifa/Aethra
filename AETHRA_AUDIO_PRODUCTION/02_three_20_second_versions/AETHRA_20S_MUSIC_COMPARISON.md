## Independence and integration

The three tracks have separate authored JSON scores, different note sequences, harmonic schedules, tempi, grooves, synthesis voices, early-reflection patterns, arrangement density and codas. They are not preset swaps of one base song. No previous unapproved music recording, arrangement or melodic phrase is reused. Shared elementary oscillator/filter utilities are synthesis tools, not a shared composition.

The approved SFX remain the common sonic foundation. Every original SFX sample is unchanged, and each version's mix uses the same effects file at unity gain. Music-only ducking and music-only headroom control create space around those sounds. The SFX are never moved to match the beats. Percussion is reduced around 12.9–13.45 seconds for the original final-branding accent. Two restrained new effects follow only the new edge light and closing settle.

Measured 1 kHz-sampled music-waveform correlations are AB 0.0685, AC 0.0127 and BC 0.0122. These values corroborate distinct signals; they are not a test of musical originality or creative quality. The independent scores document the compositional differences.
